# Dex

Big-brand marketing. Without the big-brand team.

Google Search Console only shows pages with recent traffic. The moment a page goes quiet, it drops out of every report built on that data — so nobody notices, and nobody fixes it. Dex watches the pages Search Console stops showing you.

## What it actually does

- **Site scans** — crawls your site and flags what's actually wrong: weak meta descriptions, unverified claims, broken internal links, missing structured data
- **Live scan status** — a dashboard that shows a scan in progress, not just a finished report dropped on you later
- **Competitor tracking** — add competitor domains and get flagged when their SEO posture changes, not just your own
- **GSC integration** — connects to Google Search Console for the traffic data it's actually good at, and fills in what it misses
- **Review queue** — findings come with a specific, actionable fix; you approve or reject, Dex doesn't silently rewrite your site

## Stack

Next.js, Prisma, Clerk (auth), Vercel.
